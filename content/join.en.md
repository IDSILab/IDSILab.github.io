## Join Us

The lab welcomes students from design, engineering, computer science, artificial intelligence, human factors, interaction design, and related interdisciplinary backgrounds who are interested in robotics, AI, and human–robot interaction.

Our work centers on human-centered intelligent robotics, intelligent design methods, and human–robot interaction and collaboration. We value problem awareness, self-directed learning, interdisciplinary thinking, and hands-on practice, and encourage students to connect research questions with system design, prototyping, and experimental validation.

### Students Who May Fit

- Interested in robotics, artificial intelligence, intelligent design, or human–robot interaction
- Willing to learn across disciplines and connect research questions with system implementation
- With backgrounds in design, engineering, computer science, psychology, human factors, digital media, or related areas
- Motivated to participate in prototyping, experiments, and real-world projects

### Possible Topics

- Human-centered robotics and human–robot collaboration
- Shared and adaptive autonomy, multi-robot and autonomous systems
- Multimodal human–robot interaction, human factors, and robot behavior communication
- Generative AI-assisted design and intelligent design methods
- Assistive and service robotics, complex and industrial robotics
