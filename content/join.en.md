## Join Us

The lab welcomes students from design, engineering, computer science, artificial intelligence, human factors, interaction design, and related interdisciplinary backgrounds who are interested in robotics, AI, and human–robot interaction.

Our core research field is **Human-Centered Robotics**, organized around three long-term scientific lines: Human Capability, Human Agency, and Human Role. We value problem awareness, self-directed learning, interdisciplinary thinking, and hands-on practice, and encourage students to connect real-world questions with robotic system development and experimental validation.

### Students Who May Fit

- Interested in robotics, artificial intelligence, human–robot interaction, human factors, or intelligent design
- Willing to build interdisciplinary skills in robotics, programming, and human-subject experimental methods
- With backgrounds in design, engineering, computer science, psychology, human factors, digital media, or related areas
- Motivated to work with robotic systems, prototypes, experiments, and real-world projects

### Possible Research Topics

- Human Capability and Skill in Robotic Assistance
- Human Oversight and Agency in Autonomous Robots
- Human Role in Multi-party and Multi-stakeholder Robotic Systems
- Human-centered robotics in complex, field, and industrial environments
- Human-centered robotics in assistive and service settings
