IDSI Lab

Research

We study human-centered intelligent robotic systems, with a focus on how people and increasingly capable autonomous robots can collaborate effectively in the real world.

01

Human Capability and Skill in Robotic Assistance

We investigate how robotic assistance can support task performance while sustaining human capability, learning, and effective participation over time.

1.1

Human Capability Modeling

Represent task-relevant human capability and state to support human-centered robotic assistance.

›
1.2

Adaptive Robotic Assistance

Explore assistance strategies that adapt to people, tasks, and changing levels of capability.

›
1.3

Human–Robot Co-adaptation

Study how people and robots adapt to one another through repeated interaction and collaboration.

›
02

Human Oversight and Agency in Autonomous Robots

We study how people can maintain effective understanding, supervision, intervention, and control as robotic systems become increasingly autonomous.

2.1

Human Oversight and Intervention

Support timely and effective human supervision and intervention in autonomous robotic systems.

›
2.2

Robot Intent and Uncertainty Communication

Make robot intent, uncertainty, and capability boundaries understandable during collaboration.

›
2.3

Shared and Adaptive Autonomy

Explore flexible allocation of autonomy between people and robots across tasks and situations.

›
03

Human Role in Multi-party Robotic Systems

We investigate robot interaction and behavior in environments involving multiple people with different roles, relationships, needs, and responsibilities.

3.1

Human Roles and Relationships

Study how roles, relationships, and responsibilities shape human–robot interaction.

›
3.2

Multi-party Interaction and Collaboration

Explore interaction and coordination among robots and multiple human participants.

›
3.3

Socially Situated Robot Behavior

Develop robot behavior that is responsive to social context, participation, and interaction boundaries.

IDSI Lab

研究方向

聚焦人本智能机器人系统，研究机器人能力与自主性不断增强背景下人与机器人如何在真实世界中实现有效协同。

01

机器人辅助中的人的能力与技能

研究机器人辅助如何支持任务完成，并在持续协作过程中促进人的能力保持、学习与有效参与。

1.1

人的能力建模

围绕具体任务表征人的能力与状态，为人本机器人辅助提供基础。

›
1.2

自适应机器人辅助

探索随人、任务与能力变化进行调节的机器人辅助方式。

›
1.3

人机共同适应

研究人与机器人在持续交互与协作过程中如何相互理解和共同适应。

›
02

高自主机器人中的人类监督与主体性

研究机器人自主能力持续提升过程中，人如何保持有效理解、监督、介入与控制，并形成合理的人机协同关系。

2.1

人类监督与介入

支持人在自主机器人运行过程中进行及时、有效的监督与干预。

›
2.2

机器人意图与不确定性表达

研究机器人如何表达意图、不确定性与能力边界，支持人的理解与决策。

›
2.3

共享与自适应自主

探索不同任务和情境中人与机器人之间灵活、适应性的自主权分配。

›
03

多人多角色环境中的机器人交互与系统行为

研究机器人进入家庭、工作和公共环境后，面对多个具有不同角色、关系、需求与责任的人时如何开展交互与行动。

3.1

人的角色与关系

研究角色、关系与责任如何影响人与机器人的交互和协作。

›
3.2

多人交互与协同

探索机器人与多个参与者之间的交互、协调与协作机制。

›
3.3

社会情境中的机器人行为

研究机器人如何根据社会情境、参与关系与交互边界采取适当行为。

›
