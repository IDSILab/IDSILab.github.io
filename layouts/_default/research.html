IDSI Lab · Human-Centered Robotics

Research

IDSI Lab focuses on Human-Centered Intelligent Robotic Systems (HCIRS), investigating how increasing robot capability, autonomy, and social presence reshape human capability, agency, and roles, and how robotic systems should be designed to support effective, sustainable, and human-compatible collaboration.

01

Human Capability and Skill in Robotic Assistance

How can robots help people accomplish tasks while preserving and potentially developing human capability and skill over time?

1.1

Human Capability Modeling

Model task-relevant human capability and changing states as a basis for capability-aware robotic assistance.

›
1.2

Adaptive and Skill-Preserving Assistance

Study when and how robots should assist, adapt assistance, and preserve meaningful human participation and practice.

›
1.3

Human–Robot Co-adaptation

Study how human capability and robot assistance strategies evolve through repeated interaction, learning, and collaboration.

›
02

Human Oversight and Agency in Autonomous Robots

As robot autonomy increases, how can people retain effective understanding, supervision, intervention, control, and meaningful agency?

2.1

Human Readiness and Intervention

Study when robots should request help and whether people are ready and able to intervene effectively under task risk and uncertainty.

›
2.2

Intent, Uncertainty, and Capability Communication

Investigate how robots communicate intended actions, uncertainty, capability boundaries, and requests for human involvement.

›
2.3

Shared and Adaptive Autonomy

Explore flexible allocation of autonomy across tasks, decision levels, and single- or multi-robot systems.

›
03

Human Role in Multi-party and Multi-stakeholder Robotic Systems

How should robots understand situations and act when they enter real social and work environments involving multiple people with different roles, relationships, rights, and needs?

3.1

Human Roles and Relationships

Study how human roles, relationships, and responsibilities shape robot interaction, coordination, and decision making.

›
3.2

Multi-party Interaction and Authority

Explore participation, attention, coordination, and authority when robots interact with multiple people and stakeholders.

›
3.3

Bystander-aware and Socially Situated Robotics

Study robot behavior in shared spaces with attention to bystanders, social context, interaction boundaries, and situated roles.

›
Methods &
Applications

Cross-cutting Technologies and System Methods

AI and foundation models, adaptive and shared autonomy, multi-robot systems, and robot/system design support all three research lines.

Complex, Field, and Industrial Robotics

Complex tasks, field operations, inspection, maintenance, special-purpose work, and heterogeneous robotic systems provide key validation environments.

Assistive and Service Robotics

Assistive, home, ageing, rehabilitation, and service settings provide contexts for studying human capability, agency, and roles.

IDSI Lab · Human-Centered Robotics

研究方向

IDSI Lab聚焦人本智能机器人系统（HCIRS），研究机器人能力、自主性和社会存在不断增强过程中人的能力、主体性和角色变化，以及机器人系统如何据此实现有效、可持续且与人相容的协同。

01

人机协作中的能力保持与发展

机器人如何帮助人完成任务，同时保持甚至促进人的能力和技能发展？

1.1

人的任务能力建模

面向具体任务表征人的能力与动态状态，为能力感知型机器人辅助提供基础。

›
1.2

自适应与能力保持型辅助

研究机器人何时帮助、如何调节辅助，并在完成任务的同时保留人的有效参与与实践机会。

›
1.3

人机能力共同适应

研究人的能力与机器人辅助策略如何在持续交互、学习和协作过程中共同变化。

›
02

高自主机器人中的人类监督与主体性

当机器人自主能力持续提升时，人如何保持有效的理解、监督、介入、控制与行动主体性？

2.1

人的介入状态与干预

研究机器人何时请求帮助，以及人在任务风险与不确定性下是否具备及时、有效介入的状态与能力。

›
2.2

意图、不确定性与能力边界表达

研究机器人如何表达行动意图、不确定性、能力边界及对人工参与的需求。

›
2.3

共享与自适应自主

探索不同任务、决策层级以及单机器人与多机器人系统中的自主权灵活分配。

›
03

多人多角色环境中的机器人交互与系统行为

机器人进入真实社会和工作环境后，面对多个具有不同角色、关系、权利和需求的人时，应该如何理解情境并采取行动？

3.1

人的角色与关系

研究人的角色、关系与责任如何影响机器人交互、协同与决策。

›
3.2

多人交互与控制权限

研究机器人面对多个人与多类利益相关者时的参与、注意、协调及权限关系。

›
3.3

旁观者感知与社会情境机器人

研究机器人在共享空间中如何考虑旁观者、社会情境、交互边界及其所处角色。

›
横向技术
与应用

横向技术与系统方法

AI与基础模型、共享与自适应自主、多机器人系统以及机器人/系统设计等共同支撑三条研究主线。

复杂环境、现场与工业机器人

以复杂任务、巡检维护、特种作业和异构机器人系统等作为重要研究与验证环境。

辅助与服务机器人

以辅助、家庭服务、老龄支持、康复协作等场景研究人的能力、主体性与角色问题。
